About Weekend Compass

A field guide to short getaways for people who live in the UAE — written by someone who does too.

Most travel advice is written for tourists with two free weeks. This site is the opposite: it's for residents working a Sunday-to-Thursday week who want to make a real trip out of a Friday and Saturday. Every guide is measured from here — real drive and flight times, real entry notes, and a clear answer to the only question that matters on a short trip: where should I stay?

We'd rather tell you one place is worth booking than list twenty. If something isn't good, it isn't here.

Affiliate disclosure

Weekend Compass takes part in affiliate programmes, including the Booking.com affiliate programme. This means some links on the site are affiliate links: if you click one and book a stay, we may earn a small commission. It never costs you anything extra, and it doesn't change the price you pay.

Affiliate income helps keep the site running, but it doesn't decide what we recommend. We only point to places we'd be happy to book ourselves, and our verdicts are our own. Where a page contains affiliate links, we say so at the top.
