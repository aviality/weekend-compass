Where to stay in Ras Al Khaimah for a weekend

RAK is the easiest real "getaway" from Dubai — close enough to leave after work on Thursday, different enough that it doesn't feel like the city. The only hard choice is where to base yourself. Here's how the areas break down.

Ras Al Khaimah is the northernmost emirate, and for a UAE resident it's the sweet spot: a short drive, no flight, no visa, and three genuinely different kinds of weekend depending on where you stay. You can be on a beach lounger, up a mountain, or wandering an old pearling town — sometimes across the same two days.

Quick orientation. Al Marjan Island = beach resorts. Jebel Jais = mountains and cooler air. Old RAK / Al Hamra = town, marina and value. Pick based on the weekend you want, not the map.

The three areas, and who each suits

Al Marjan Island — for a switch-off beach weekend

A cluster of man-made islands lined with resorts. This is where most people stay, and for good reason: you park once, and everything — beach, pool, dinner — is on site. Best for couples who want to do nothing, and families who want the kids occupied.

Jebel Jais — for cooler air and a view

The UAE's highest mountain. Temperatures run several degrees below the coast, which makes it a genuinely different trip in the warmer months. Fewer places to stay up here, so book ahead — and note you'll really want a car.

Marina and lagoon stays, plus easy reach of the old town and the abandoned village of Al Jazirah Al Hamra. Usually better value than Al Marjan, and a good base if you want to actually see the emirate rather than just its beach.

An easy two-day plan

Thursday evening: drive up after work, check in, dinner at the resort or marina.
Friday: slow morning on the beach or by the pool; afternoon drive up to Jebel Jais for the cooler air and sunset.
Saturday: late checkout, brunch, and an easy drive home before the evening.

Getting there & when to go

RAK is roughly 45–75 minutes from most of Dubai and about two hours from Abu Dhabi. The comfortable season runs October to April; in peak summer, lean toward Jebel Jais or a resort where you'll mostly be poolside. Weekends and UAE public holidays fill the best resorts — book early for those.

Common questions

How far is Ras Al Khaimah from Dubai?

Around 45–75 minutes by car from most of Dubai, depending on your starting point and traffic. From Abu Dhabi, closer to two hours.

Do you need a car?

For a resort-only weekend on Al Marjan you can rely on taxis. If you want Jebel Jais or plan to mix beach and mountain, a car makes it much easier.

Is RAK worth it over Fujairah?

RAK has more resorts and the mountain; Fujairah is quieter and a shorter drive from some areas. We compare them directly in a separate guide.

When is the cheapest time to go?

Rates soften in the hotter months (roughly May–September). If you're heat-tolerant or staying poolside, that's when the deals are.
