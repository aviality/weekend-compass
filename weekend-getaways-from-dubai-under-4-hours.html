Weekend getaways from Dubai under 4 hours' flight

A short flight turns a two-day weekend into a real change of scenery. These are the destinations close enough to leave after work Friday and be back for Monday — sorted by how long you'll actually be in the air.

The trick to a good UAE weekend abroad is honesty about flight time. Anything over about four hours each way eats too much of a two-day trip. Below, every destination is measured from Dubai, with the entry situation for UAE residents flagged up front — always reconfirm visa rules for your own passport before booking, as they change.

How to read this. Flight times are approximate direct-flight durations from DXB. "Entry" is a general pointer for common residents' passports — your nationality is what matters, so verify it.

The stand-outs

Oman — the no-brainer first trip

Barely over an hour to Muscat, and a completely different pace. Mountains, wadis, forts and an old-Arabia calm the UAE has mostly built over. In summer, Salalah's khareef season turns the south green — a genuinely surprising July escape.

Georgia & Azerbaijan — Europe-ish for the price of a short hop

Three hours puts you in the Caucasus: green mountains, old towns, wine country and cool air in spring and autumn. Great value once you land, and an easy visa situation for many residents.

How to fit it in a long weekend

Fly out Friday evening after work, land the same night, and you've got the weekend plus a Sunday-evening return before the Monday workday. Pack carry-on only — checked bags cost you an hour you don't have on a short trip. For anything with an e-visa, apply the moment your flights are booked, not the week before.
